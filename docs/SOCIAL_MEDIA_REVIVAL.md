# VISUALIZE Sports Social Media Revival Playbook

## Executive Summary

**The Challenge:** VISUALIZE Sports' social media has been dormant for 2+ years. In a 1-2 year wind-down phase, the question isn't "how do we build a massive following" — it's "what's the minimum viable social presence that drives Amazon sales?"

**The Strategy:** Focus on **Instagram + TikTok** with golf-specific content that showcases products in use. Abandoned Facebook and X unless zero-effort opportunities arise. Prioritize content that works as both organic reach AND repurposable for Amazon A+ content.

**Time Investment Target:** 2-3 hours/week maximum

## Content Strategy

### Content Pillars

Build all content around these 4 themes:

#### 1. 🎯 Training Tips (40% of content)
*"How to use alignment sticks to fix your slice"*

- Quick tips using VISUALIZE products
- Before/after swing comparisons
- Practice drills
- Format: Reels, TikTok, carousel

**Why it works:** Provides value, demonstrates product utility, shareable

#### 2. ⛳ Golf Lifestyle (25% of content)
*"Perfect morning on the range"*

- Course beauty shots with products visible
- Golfer POV content
- Seasonal golf content (spring prep, fall rounds)
- Format: Static posts, Stories, Reels

**Why it works:** Builds aspirational brand, easy to produce

#### 3. 🏆 Customer Proof (20% of content)
*"Look what @golferfan shared with us!"*

- Repost customer content (UGC)
- Reviews/testimonials as graphics
- Transformation stories
- Format: Reposts, Stories, carousel

**Why it works:** Social proof, minimal production effort, builds community

#### 4. 🛒 Product Showcase (15% of content)
*"Our alignment sticks are built to last — here's why"*

- Product close-ups
- Feature highlights
- Bundle deals
- Amazon link in bio callout
- Format: Static, carousel, Stories

**Why it works:** Direct conversion, necessary for commerce

## Content Creation System

### Batch Production (Monthly)

Spend 2-3 hours one day per month to create content backlog:

1. **Record 10 short videos** (15-30 sec each) using products
2. **Take 20 product/lifestyle photos** 
3. **Create 4 carousel templates** in Canva
4. **Write 10 caption variations**

This gives ~1 month of content to schedule.

### Low-Effort Content Ideas

**Zero-production content:**
- Repost customer content (ask permission or use public tags)
- Share golf memes with product mention
- Quote graphics from golf pros
- "What are you working on this week?" engagement posts
- Poll stickers in Stories

**Minimal-production content:**
- Product flat-lays (phone camera, natural light)
- Screen recordings of Amazon reviews
- "Day in the life" Stories at the range
- Sunset/sunrise course shots

### User-Generated Content (UGC) Strategy

**How to encourage UGC:**
1. Add card insert in Amazon packaging: "Share your setup @visualizesports"
2. Feature customers prominently (they'll share to their audience)
3. Run simple monthly contest: "Best training setup wins free product"
4. Create branded hashtag: #VISUALIZEgolf

**How to find existing UGC:**
- Search Instagram for brand mentions/tags
- Search Amazon reviews for customer photos
- Google Image search product names

## Amazon Traffic Strategy

### The Link-in-Bio Bridge

**Instagram/TikTok → Amazon conversion path:**

```
Content → Interest → Bio Link → Linktree/Landing → Amazon
```

**Linktree Setup:**
1. Shop Best Sellers (→ Amazon storefront)
2. Alignment Sticks (→ Top product)
3. Training Bundles (→ Bundle listing)
4. On Sale Now (→ Current promotion)
5. About VISUALIZE (→ Optional brand story)

### Call-to-Action Strategy

**Soft CTAs (for value content):**
- "Link in bio if you want to try this drill"
- "Everything I use is linked in bio"
- "Comment 'LINK' and I'll DM you"

**Direct CTAs (for product content):**
- "Shop the [product] — link in bio"
- "50 left in stock — link in bio"
- "Prime shipping if you order today"

### Attribution Tracking

**Challenge:** Social → Amazon tracking is notoriously difficult.

**Solutions:**
1. **Amazon Attribution** (if available)
   - Create unique tracking links for social
   - Measure click-through from specific campaigns

2. **Correlation Analysis**
   - Track daily social engagement vs. Amazon sales
   - Look for spikes after viral content

3. **Promo Codes**
   - Create social-exclusive codes
   - Track redemption rates

4. **Ask in Reviews**
   - "How did you hear about us?" 
   - Monitor new review sources

## Wind-Down Considerations

**As inventory decreases:**
1. Shift content to "limited stock" messaging
2. Consider "farewell" narrative ("Last batch ever")
3. Archive valuable content for potential brand sale
4. Prepare to deactivate gracefully (not abandon)

**Exit options:**
- Sell social accounts with brand
- Transfer followers to new venture
- Archive and deactivate

---

*This playbook is designed for maximum impact with minimum ongoing effort. The goal is to extract value from social during the wind-down phase, not build an empire. Every hour spent should drive measurable Amazon traffic or build brand equity for potential exit.*
